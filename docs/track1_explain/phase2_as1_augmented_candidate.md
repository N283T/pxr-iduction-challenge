# Phase 2 AS1-augmented top500 candidate

Built on 2026-06-10 JST.

This is the first actual Phase 2 improvement candidate in this branch. It is
not just a diagnostic: it trains the strong top500 TabPFN recipe with released
AS1 labels added to the training pool.

## What was trained

- Feature: `cheme_2d_full_boltz_log2fc_pred_seed10ens`
- Feature dimension before selection: 2103
- Top-K selector: LightGBM gain on train + AS1 labels
- K: 500
- Model: TabPFN v3, `n_estimators=8`, `softmax_temperature=0.7`
- Training rows: 4140 original train + 253 released AS1 labels = 4393
- Blind prediction target: AS2 260 compounds

## Read

The direct AS1-augmented model fits AS1 strongly, but it moves AS2 too far from
the id55 anchor for a blind final candidate:

- model-only AS1 MAE: 0.0944
- AS2 mean absolute shift vs id55: 0.1255
- AS2 p90 absolute shift vs id55: 0.2510
- AS2 max absolute shift vs id55: 0.6299

The direct model is therefore a useful new axis, not a safe submission by
itself.

The conservative id55 blends are more plausible. AS2-only shift summary:

| candidate | AS2 mean abs shift | AS2 p90 abs shift | AS2 max abs shift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `a0p1` | 0.0126 | 0.0251 | 0.0630 |
| `a0p2` | 0.0251 | 0.0502 | 0.1260 |
| `a0p3` | 0.0377 | 0.0753 | 0.1890 |
| `a0p4` | 0.0502 | 0.1004 | 0.2520 |
| `a0p5` | 0.0628 | 0.1255 | 0.3150 |

`a0p1` is the first candidate worth deeper review. `a0p2` is still plausible
but starts moving the AS2 risk slices noticeably. Larger alphas are mainly
diagnostic unless additional validation supports the AS1-augmented axis.

## Guardrail

`submission_preflight.py` reports HOLD for the labels-as1 CSVs because replacing
AS1 rows with released labels is a large whole-file shift relative to id55. For
these Phase 2 artifacts, the relevant safety check is AS2-only movement plus the
interim full-test sanity check, not the raw all-513 preflight verdict.
